04 / THE FOUNDER CREATION SYSTEM

The
Founder Creation System.

Africa 2060 works backwards from company opportunities rather than forwards from courses: what companies can be created, what founders are required, what capabilities must be developed, and how value returns into future founder creation.

  1. 01TRAIN

    Build sector capability plus entrepreneurship, financial literacy, AI fluency and company-building fundamentals.

  2. 02MATCH

    Combine complementary people across Vocational, Technical and Operational pathways.

  3. 03FORM

    Assemble teams around real opportunities and incorporate companies.

  4. 04FUND

    Allocate equity and seed resources through the ownership architecture.

  5. 05SCALE

    Support execution, markets, customers, systems and growth.

  6. 06REINVEST

    Recycle Initiative value into future cohorts, companies and ownership.

14 / OPEN ITEMS

Not yet
decided.

The current framework deliberately records unresolved governance and implementation questions rather than presenting them as settled policy.

  1. Formal quorum threshold for a valid Senate vote.
  2. Detailed founder agreement and vesting schedule terms (cliff, vesting period, leaver provisions).
  3. Full policy for teams that do not reach viability — re-matching vs. dissolution, and treatment of any equity already allocated.
  4. Legal entity structure that holds equity across the venture portfolio.
  5. Per-branch cohort sizing and frequency — no fixed trainee count has been set as policy.
  6. Seat count and selection process for Regional Senators as the Initiative expands beyond its initial market.
  7. Term length and succession process for all Senate seats.
  8. Selection and appointment process for Sector Heads — Senate-approved, Senator-appointed, or open application.
  9. Compensation and/or equity treatment for Sector Heads and Branch teams.
  10. Quantified criteria for advancing between sourcing-strategy phases, beyond the qualitative triggers in Part IV.
  11. Formal criteria for activating, merging, pausing or retiring sectors/Branches under the Three-Track Architecture.
