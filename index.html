02 / THE REFRAMEFROM SKILLS TO OWNERSHIP

Move from workforce development to founder creation.

The central question changes when capability is treated as an input to ownership. People do not enter the system simply to become employable. They develop the capability to solve problems, work with complementary founders and build organisations around opportunity.

CAPABILITYSkills become founder capacity.Vocational, Technical and Operational pathways develop complementary ways of making, engineering and organising.
OWNERSHIPCapability becomes company formation.The intended destination is not training completion. It is applied problem-solving, team formation and company creation.
HORIZON2060 is the operating horizon.The architecture is designed to renew as sectors, technology and African markets change.
